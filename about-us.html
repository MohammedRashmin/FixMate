Our Story

Founded in 2020, FixMate started as a simple idea: to make skilled labor services accessible to everyone. What began as a small team of passionate individuals has grown into the country's most trusted platform for home services.

We believe that quality home services shouldn't be a luxury. That's why we've built a platform that connects customers with verified, skilled professionals at affordable prices.
